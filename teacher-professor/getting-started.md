# Getting Started

### **1. Logging into RealLives**

To access the RealLives Simulation Dashboard, follow these steps:

1. **Visit the Login Page:** Navigate to the official RealLives Simulation website or app provided by your institution.
2. **Enter Credentials:** Input the teacher/professor login details (email and password) provided to you.
3. **Access the Dashboard:** Click the **“Log In”** button to enter your personalized dashboard.

> **Note:** If you encounter login issues, ensure your credentials are correct or use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password.

***

### **2. Exploring the Dashboard**

The RealLives Simulation Dashboard is designed to be intuitive and easy to use. The main components include:

* **Sidebar Menu:** Located on the left, it allows navigation between features such as **My Journey** and **Manage Classes**.
* **Top Bar:** Displays license availability, account settings, and notifications.
* **Main Panel:** The central workspace where you interact with the selected feature.

***

### **3. Setting Up Your Profile**

After logging in, ensure your profile is set up correctly:

1. **Access Account Settings:** Click your initials or profile icon in the top-right corner.
2. **Update Information:** Add or update details such as name, email, and institution name.
3. **Save Changes:** Make sure to save any changes before exiting.

***

### **4. Checking License Availability**

You can view the number of available licenses at the top of the dashboard. Ensure there are enough licenses for your students:

* **Available Licenses:** Displays total and allocated licenses (e.g., 500/500).
* **Managing Licenses:** Click on the sidebar to review or request additional licenses.

***

### **5. Changing Language**

The RealLives Simulation Dashboard supports multiple languages to cater to a diverse user base. To change the language, follow these steps:

**Select Preferred Language:** Choose from the available options (e.g., English, Korean, Swedish).

> **Tip:** If your preferred language is not available, contact the support team to request additional language options.

***

### **6. Managing Notifications**

Stay updated on student progress, assignments, and system updates. Here's how to manage notifications:

1. **Access Notifications:** Click the **bell icon** in the top-right corner of the dashboard to view recent alerts.
2. **Read Notifications:** Notifications include updates on student activities, assignment reminders, and system messages.
3. **Organize Notifications:** Mark notifications as read or clear them to keep your dashboard tidy.

***
